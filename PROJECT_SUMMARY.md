# AI Revenue Autopilot

## What is this project?

AI Revenue Autopilot is an AI-powered assistant that turns a business growth goal into a guided sales workflow. A user describes their ideal customers, and the application helps discover prospects, evaluate their fit, personalize outreach, understand replies, and recommend the next action.

## Purpose

The project aims to reduce repetitive sales research and follow-up work so teams can spend more time having useful conversations with potential customers. It explains why a prospect is a good fit and keeps the user in control of important actions.

## How it works

**Business goal → AI plan → Graph8 search → AI qualification and scoring → Personalized message → Graph8 outreach → Reply analysis → Recommended action → User approval → Graph8 booking**

For example, a user asks: “Find 50 SaaS companies in the USA that could benefit from our AI automation platform.” The system identifies opportunities and helps move the strongest prospect toward a meeting.

## What makes it different?

**Our application is the AI brain. Graph8 is the revenue engine.**

OpenAI provides planning, scoring, personalization, and reply analysis. Graph8 provides prospect data, contact records, outreach infrastructure, and meeting bookings. We build an intelligence layer on top of Graph8 rather than rebuilding a CRM.

## Technology

- **Frontend:** React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, and shadcn/ui components.
- **Backend:** Python FastAPI for AI intelligence and orchestration.
- **AI:** OpenAI for structured decisions and generated messages.
- **Revenue infrastructure:** Graph8 APIs.

## Hackathon demonstration

The demo simulates **128 prospects found, 34 qualified, a top lead scored 92/100, and an interested reply that leads to an approved meeting action**. These are demonstration figures, not measured business results. Live execution requires configured AI credentials and Graph8 outreach resources; the current live workflow follows one top opportunity.
